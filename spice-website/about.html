Our Story

From a small family shop in Mumbai's historic spice market to a global supplier trusted by 500+ clients worldwide.

Global Spice Traders was founded in 1998 by Rajesh Patel, a third-generation spice merchant who grew up among the vibrant colors and aromas of Crawford Market. What began as a modest operation supplying local restaurants and retailers has evolved into an international trading house with direct relationships across 40+ spice-producing regions.

Our journey has been guided by a simple philosophy: the best spices come from knowing the land, the farmers, and the traditions behind every harvest. We don't just trade commodities—we build partnerships that span generations.

Today, our team of 45 specialists manages sourcing, quality control, logistics, and client relations from our Mumbai headquarters and regional offices in Dubai, London, and New York. Every batch that leaves our facilities carries our commitment to purity, transparency, and fair trade.

Our Values

The principles that guide every decision we make, from farm to warehouse to your door.

Authenticity First

We source directly from origin, maintaining the integrity of each spice's unique terroir and traditional cultivation methods.

Uncompromising Quality

ISO 17025 accredited testing on every lot. We reject anything that doesn't meet our exacting standards for purity and potency.

Fair Partnerships

Long-term agreements with farmer cooperatives ensure fair prices, technical support, and community investment.

Reliable Delivery

Temperature-controlled logistics, real-time tracking, and complete documentation for seamless global shipping.

Transparency

Full traceability from farm to container. Certificate of Analysis, origin certificates, and processing records for every shipment.

Innovation

Custom blending, novel processing techniques, and sustainable packaging solutions for evolving market needs.
