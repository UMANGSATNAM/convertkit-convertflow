# 02 FEATURE SPECIFICATION — ALL 7 PILLARS

## PILLAR 1: Guided Setup Wizard
The entry point for every new merchant. Reduces time-to-value to under 30 minutes. Replaces the need for a Shopify developer.
- **Step-by-step store checklist (P0)**: Visual progress bar showing store readiness %. Each task links directly to the relevant section.
- **AI store setup assistant (P0)**: Merchant enters niche + product type. App auto-configures recommended settings, colors, section layout, and enabled features.
- **Niche-based page templates (P0)**: Pre-built pages for 8 niches (64 templates total). One click to inject directly into the store.
- **Contextual help tooltips (P1)**: Every setting has a '?' tooltip explaining what it does and why it matters. Beginner never needs to Google.
- **Setup video walkthroughs (P2)**: 30-second embedded video for each major section.
- **Niche auto-configuration (P0)**: Selecting 'Jewellery' auto-enables: hallmark badge, occasion pages, ring sizer, gold/silver filter.

## PILLAR 2: Conversion Booster Suite
6 features that replace Urgency Bear, Flair, Sales Pop, and Sticky Cart. Saves merchants Rs.5,000-8,000/month alone.
- **Countdown Timers (HIGH IMPACT)**: Real deadline timers on PDPs. Evergreen (resets per session) or fixed date. Customizable colors and display style.
- **Stock Scarcity Counter (HIGH IMPACT)**: 'Only 3 left in stock' auto-shown when inventory drops below merchant-configured threshold. Live from Shopify inventory API.
- **Sticky ATC Button (HIGH IMPACT)**: Add to cart stays visible on scroll. Mobile-first. Single biggest mobile CRO win. +10-15% mobile conversion.
- **Free Shipping Progress Bar (HIGH IMPACT)**: 'Add Rs.199 more for free shipping.' Updates live in cart as items added. Proven AOV booster +15-25%.
- **Live Visitor Count (MEDIUM)**: '18 people viewing this right now.' Configurable with real or simulated counts. Creates FOMO effectively.
- **Sales Popup Notifications (MEDIUM)**: 'Priya from Mumbai just bought this.' Pulls from real recent orders. Configurable delay and duration.
*(Delivered as a single optimized JavaScript bundle via Theme App Extension. Total LCP impact under 50ms.)*

## PILLAR 3: Trust & Social Proof Builder
- **Reviews with Photo/Video (P0)**: Import via CSV. Manual add. Auto-request email after delivery. Star rating widget on PDP.
- **Trust Badge Builder (P0)**: Drag-and-drop below ATC. Presets: COD Available, Free Returns, Secure Payment, 100% Authentic, Made in India.
- **WhatsApp Chat Widget (INDIA MUST HAVE)**: Floating WA button with pre-filled message template.
- **Pincode Delivery Checker (INDIA MUST HAVE)**: On PDP — 'Enter pincode to check delivery date.' Uses Shiprocket API. Reduces checkout drop-off massively.
- **Press / Media Logo Bar (P1)**: Auto-scrolling 'As Seen In' logo strip. Upload logos or choose from presets.
- **Customer Count Milestone (P1)**: '50,000+ happy customers' auto-pulls from Shopify order count. Updates live.

## PILLAR 4: AOV & Upsell Engine
- **Frequently Bought Together (HIGH IMPACT)**: Manual or AI-suggested product bundles on PDP. 'Complete the look' UI. One-click add to cart.
- **Bundle Builder (HIGH IMPACT)**: Buy 2 get 10% off, Buy 3 get 20% off. Shown on PDP. Uses Shopify Discount API natively.
- **Post-Purchase Upsell (HIGH IMPACT)**: After order confirmed — one-click offer. Customer does NOT re-enter payment. Highest-converting upsell point.
- **In-Cart Upsell (P1)**: Cart drawer shows 'You might also like' with one-click add. No page reload required.
- **Volume Discount Tiers (P1)**: Auto-apply discounts at cart level. Natively integrated with Shopify Discount API.
- **Subscribe & Save (P2)**: Recurring orders for consumables (supplements, skincare, coffee). Shopify Subscriptions API.

## PILLAR 5: Recovery & Retention
- **Abandoned Cart Recovery (P0)**: Auto WhatsApp + email 3-message sequence. 1hr reminder, 6hr urgency, 24hr discount. Tracks recovery rate.
- **Exit Intent Popup (HIGH IMPACT)**: Detects mouse leaving viewport (desktop) or scroll-up trigger (mobile). Shows discount or newsletter offer.
- **Email Capture Popups (P1)**: Timed or scroll-triggered. '10% off for your email.' Syncs with Klaviyo and Mailchimp via webhook.
- **WhatsApp Broadcast (INDIA MUST HAVE)**: Send promotional messages to opted-in customers. Sale announcements, restock alerts, new arrivals.
- **Winback Campaigns (P2)**: Auto-trigger for customers with no purchase in 30/60/90 days. Configurable message templates.

## PILLAR 6: India-First Features — Competitive Moat
These features are the primary competitive moat. No Western all-in-one app offers COD management + GST invoicing + Shiprocket together.
- **COD Order Management (INDIA UNIQUE)**: COD confirmation via WhatsApp. NDR management dashboard. Fake order filter using address/phone heuristics. Reduces RTO losses.
- **GST Invoice Generator (INDIA UNIQUE)**: Auto-generate GST-compliant invoices. GSTIN, HSN code, CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown. PDF download and email to customer.
- **Shiprocket / Delhivery Integration (INDIA UNIQUE)**: One-click shipping label generation. Rate comparison across 20+ couriers. Auto tracking update via WhatsApp.
- **Hindi / Regional Language Toggle (INDIA UNIQUE)**: Key PDP sections translateable to Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Marathi. Toggle per product or site-wide.
- **UPI Payment Nudge (INDIA UNIQUE)**: Prominent UPI payment option at checkout. Integrates with Razorpay UPI flow. Increases payment success rate.
- **COD Confirmation Flow (INDIA UNIQUE)**: WhatsApp message: 'Confirm your order of Rs.X? Reply YES.' Auto-cancel unconfirmed orders after 24hrs.

## PILLAR 7: Analytics & Smart Dashboard
- **Real-Time Profit Calculator (P0)**: Per-order P&L; pulling live data from Razorpay (PG fees), Shiprocket (shipping), Facebook Ads (CAC), Shopify (order value).
- **Facebook Ads Integration (P0)**: OAuth connect to Facebook Business Manager. Pull campaign spend, ROAS, CPM, CPC. Shows ad cost per order automatically.
- **Conversion Funnel Report (P0)**: Visual funnel: Sessions > PDP Views > ATC > Checkout > Purchased. Shows exact drop-off % at each stage.
- **AI Recommendations Engine (P1)**: 'Your cart abandonment is 78%. Enable exit intent popup to recover Rs.12,000/month.' Actionable insights.
- **Feature Performance Scores (P1)**: Each enabled feature shows attributed revenue impact. Gamifies CRO for beginners.
- **Heatmap-Lite (P2)**: Click/tap tracking on PDP. Shows hotspots without any third-party tool.
